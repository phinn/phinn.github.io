DayStitch — Support & Privacy

About DayStitch

DayStitch turns what your phone already knows — places you visited, photos you took, your steps and drives, your calendar — into one written story of your day. No typing. Every night the story is already there, waiting for you to read and keep.

Frequently Asked

  • Where is my data processed? Entirely on your device. The story is composed by on-device logic only — nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to any server.
  • Does DayStitch modify my photos or calendar? No. It reads only what iOS already shows you, and never edits or deletes anything.
  • Can I export? Story cards can be saved to your photo library; entries stay in the app's local storage.

Email: support@daystitch.app

Privacy Policy

DayStitch collects no data. Period.

  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • All data (locations, photos, motion, calendar events, stories) stays on your device.
  • We have no servers. There is nothing to breach, nothing to sell.
  • Permissions (Location / Photos / Motion / Calendar) are requested only to compose your daily story, and you can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings.

DayStitch — 支持与隐私

关于 DayStitch

DayStitch 把手机已经知道的事——你去过的地方、拍下的照片、步数与行程、日历日程——自动缝成一天的故事。一个字都不用写,每晚故事已就位,等你翻阅。

常见问题

  • 数据在哪里处理? 全部在你的设备上。故事完全由端侧逻辑生成,不上传、不联网。
  • 会改动我的照片或日历吗? 不会。只读取 iOS 本来就展示给你的内容,永不编辑或删除任何东西。
  • 可以导出吗? 故事卡片可保存到相册;条目保存在应用本地存储中。

隐私政策

DayStitch 不收集任何数据。

  • 无账号、无注册、无统计、无追踪、无广告。
  • 所有数据(位置、照片、运动、日程、故事)都留在你的设备上。
  • 我们没有服务器——没有可被攻破的库,也没有可出售的数据。
  • 权限(位置/照片/运动/日历)仅用于生成当天故事,可在 iOS 设置中随时关闭。
