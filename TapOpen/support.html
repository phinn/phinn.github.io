TapOpen — One Big Button · Passwords for Parents, One Tap · v1.0

How it works

  • Parent phone: the whole screen is one big button. Tap it — the door opens (website opens, login is filled from the secure vault automatically).
  • Family phone: tap the version number at the bottom of the parent screen 5 times to enter Family Setup. Add doors (name, website, password), then use “Copy setup for their phone” and send the copied text to the parent by any messaging app. On the parent phone, enter Family Setup and tap “Paste setup from a message”.
  • To return to the parent screen, tap the version number 5 times again.

Do you see my passwords?

No. Passwords are stored only in your device’s Keychain and never leave it. TapOpen has no accounts and no servers. “Family setup” travels the same way as any message you would send yourself — copy, send, paste — and is erased from the clipboard 10 minutes after copying or right after it is imported.

Troubleshooting

  • “No doors yet” screen: the parent device hasn’t received a setup yet. Enter Family Setup (5 taps on the version number) and paste, or add a door manually.
  • Door opens but not logged in: the stored password may not match the site. Update it in Family Setup.
  • Paste does nothing: the setup text expires 10 minutes after copying. Ask the sender to copy again.
