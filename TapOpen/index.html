TapOpen — Support & Privacy

About TapOpen

TapOpen gives an aging parent one big button for the websites your family sets up for them — a video call, the pharmacy, the family album. A family member configures everything on their own phone and sends it over in a message. The parent just taps. Passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain and are never shown on screen.

Contact

Email: support@tapopenapp.com

Privacy Policy

TapOpen collects no data. Period.

  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • All data (door names, web addresses, passwords) stays on your device, in the app's own storage and the iOS Keychain.
  • Configuration travels device-to-device only through channels you choose yourself (e.g. Messages, WeChat) — the app never uploads anything.
  • We do not have servers. There is nothing to breach, nothing to sell.

隐私政策

TapOpen 不收集任何数据。

  • 无账号、无注册、无统计、无追踪、无广告。
  • 所有数据(门的名称、网址、密码)只存在你手机本地的应用存储和 iOS 钥匙串里。
  • 配置只在两台手机之间通过你自己选择的渠道(短信、微信等)传递——应用本身不上传任何东西。
  • 我们没有服务器。无数据可泄露，无数据可买卖。
