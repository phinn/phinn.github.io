TapOpen Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-21 · v1.0 · support@tapopenapp.com

TapOpen is a family helper that shows one large button on a parent's device to open a website chosen by a family member. We collect no data. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

Where your data lives

  • On the device only. Website addresses ("doors") are stored locally on the parent's device.
  • Keychain. A password saved with a door is stored in the iOS Keychain. It is never displayed on screen and never included in setup exports.
  • Clipboard. Setup sharing uses the device clipboard: the sending device copies a setup package, and the receiving device imports it and wipes the clipboard immediately. Packages expire after 10 minutes. TapOpen never transmits them itself.

Permissions

  • Default browser — opened when a parent taps a door button.
  • Clipboard — read only when someone chooses "Paste setup from a message", and cleared right after import.

Children

TapOpen never collects data from any user, of any age.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the App Store description and an updated policy at this URL.
