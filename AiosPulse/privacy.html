Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

AiosPulse 不收集任何数据。

  • 不收集:无账号系统、无分析 SDK、无广告、无追踪器
  • 存储:App Store Connect 数据快照与 AI 配置只保存在你的设备本地(快照在本地 Documents,AI Key 存系统钥匙串)
  • 网络:仅在你主动连接自己的 App Store Connect 账号或调用你自选的 AI 服务时,由你的设备直接向对应服务发起请求;开发者不经手、不中转、不存储任何数据
  • 权限:不申请定位、通讯录、麦克风、相机
  • AI 功能:可选。不配置则所有本地分析照常可用;配置后请求直接从你的设备发往你指定的服务商(如 OpenAI 兼容端点或本地 Ollama)

AiosPulse collects no data.

  • No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, no trackers
  • Storage: your App Store Connect snapshots and AI settings stay on your device only (snapshots in local Documents; the AI key lives in the system Keychain)
  • Network is used only when you connect to your own App Store Connect account or call an AI provider of your choice — requests go directly from your device to those services. The developer never relays or stores any of it
  • Permissions: no location, contacts, microphone, or camera
  • AI features are optional. Without configuration all local analysis still works; once configured, requests go straight from your device to the provider you specify (an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local Ollama)
